Metrics: target passage hit (any gold group represented) and complete evidence

Answer experiment: every casual_a question, fixed in advance (24 pairs), same
Gemma E4B, same production source packer, no question rewrite, no chat history or
follow-up generation, same deterministic sampling settings and output budget.

testing the entire application workflow. Alternate arms, preserve requests and
responses, capture clipping, failures and timings. Score against frozen criteria:
0 = core wrong/unsupported/refusal; 1 = partially correct; 2 = both criteria met
without a material false assertion. Also flag whether supplied original sources
support the answer. Blind arm labels in the review packet. Manual scoring by the
coordinating assistant is independent of the PR author, not an independent human
judge. Make the full packet available for audit.

Decision: compare net useful-answer gain with measured extra upfront time. Report
amortized preparation seconds per query at 10, 100 and 1000 queries, and calculate

do not recommend enabling by default on the strength of retrieval alone.
